The philosophy behind the method

At first I thought it would be manipulative to sell people what they said they needed, even if I thought they didn’t actually need it. But I’ve realised that it’s not true. It’s respectful.

People know what they need for themselves, better than anyone else does. Like you and I know what we need, better than anyone else does, and we appreciate when our needs are met as precisely as possible. And we pay for that.

Successful businesses do the same. They understand the needs of their clients and they meet them. And the more precisely they do this, the more financially successful they become.

I help you do the same.

Inspired by a classic, applied to business

The sea theme here is based on Homer’s Odyssey, and the foundation of my method:

When a sailor says he needs to get home to his family, believe him. And if he asks for your help, help him with exactly that.

Don’t be led astray by non-committal desires. They’re like mermaids: they’ll mislead and drown you. The foundation of your business is a human need, met, and you rewarded for it.
